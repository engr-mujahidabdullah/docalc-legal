Summary

  • Patient data never leaves your device. Values you enter into calculators, saved patient profiles, results history, cell counts and camera images stay on your phone or browser and are never sent to us.
  • You can use every free tool without an account.
  • If you create an account we store your email address, an optional name and role, your subscription status and, for Docalc Pro, your synced favourite and recent tools.
  • We do not show ads, sell data or use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

Who we are

Docalc is published by Metcon Devs (“we”, “us”), the data controller for the information described here. Contact: docalc@metconhitech.com.

What we do not collect

Patient information or calculator inputs; your location; contacts; photos (the colony-counter camera is processed on the device only); advertising identifiers; health data about you.

Legal bases

Where data-protection law such as the GDPR applies, we rely on performance of a contract (providing your account and subscription), legitimate interests (security and fraud prevention) and legal obligations (for example tax records kept by Google for purchases).

Service providers

We use these processors, under contract, only to run Docalc:

  • Supabase — authentication, database and file storage.
  • Google — Google Play Billing (payments are handled by Google; we never see your card details) and Play Integrity.
  • Vercel — hosting of the Docalc website.
  • An email delivery provider — sending sign-in codes.

Data may be processed outside your country; providers use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Retention

We keep account data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, profile, subscription records held by us, device registrations and synced data are deleted immediately from our live systems and from backups within 30 days. Google keeps its own purchase records under its policies. Security logs are kept for up to 90 days.

Your rights

You can access and correct your data in the app, export it on request, and delete your account at any time in the app (Saved → Account → Delete account) or via this page. Depending on where you live you may also object to or restrict processing and complain to your data-protection authority. Email docalc@metconhitech.com; we reply within 30 days.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS only). Database access is restricted per user with row-level security, Pro is verified server-side with Google, and secrets are never stored in the app.

Children

Docalc is intended for healthcare professionals and students aged 18 or over. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the effective date; significant changes will also be notified in the app.
