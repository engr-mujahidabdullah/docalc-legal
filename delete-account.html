How to delete your account and the data we hold

PrivacyTermsDelete accountContact

Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Docalc and go to Saved → Account (or tap the account button on Home).
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm Delete permanently.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Request by email

If you can’t access the app, email docalc@metconhitech.com from the address registered to your account with the subject “Delete my Docalc account”. We confirm and complete deletion within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your account and email address, optional name and role
  • Subscription records we hold, device registrations and synced favourites/recent tools

Removed from live systems immediately and from backups within 30 days. Data stored only on your device (patient values, history, counts) stays on the device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Google keeps its own purchase records under its policies.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Docalc.
